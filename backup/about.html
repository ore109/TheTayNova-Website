About TayNova

TayNova is a curated marketing brand focused on home decor, lifestyle inspiration, and digital creativity. The goal is to help everyday people design beautiful, functional living spaces while discovering affordable finds through trusted platforms like Temu, Amazon, and Pinterest. TayNova combines aesthetic design, product curation, and educational content to inspire cozy living — blending style, comfort, and practicality for every home.

TayNova creates calm lifestyle resets using home, routine, and intentional living — designed for people who want peace, focus, and structure in real life.

Vision

To become a go-to online space for discovering curated home decor, lifestyle ideas, and digital resources — where inspiration meets affordability and creativity thrives.

Mission

To make home styling and digital creativity accessible to everyone by sharing budget-friendly products, design inspiration, and easy tutorials that elevate daily living.
