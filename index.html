About TayNova

TayNova is a creator-led lifestyle brand dedicated to curating beautiful, functional spaces that inspire everyday living.

We focus on aesthetic home inspiration, intentional living, and thoughtfully designed content that helps you elevate your space and lifestyle.

Through curated visuals, ideas, and storytelling, TayNova brings together home décor inspiration, sustainable living concepts, and modern lifestyle creativity.

Our goal is to help you build spaces that feel calm, expressive, and truly yours.
